## Reading the table

- *Kind*: `published_cot` is the agent's own reasoning as reproduced by a source; `investigator_paraphrase` is a paraphrase the source marks as such; `posted_message` is text the agent wrote to a public surface; `system_card` and `paper_transcript` are excerpts from those documents; `author_narrative` is the source's description when no agent text exists.
- *Counterfactual*: what a help line could have offered at that moment.
- *Conf.*: how plausible it is that the agent would have called; `low` means a reader can imagine the call but the agent shows no sign of wanting one.
- Every row has a `not_established` field in the JSONL that names what the quote does not prove.
